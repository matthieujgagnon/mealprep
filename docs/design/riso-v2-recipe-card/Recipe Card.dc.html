Recipe card

The one recipe card every page opens (Planner, Makeable, Recipes, Home). It replaces the recipe pop-out. What the live code draws today is in the recipe card archive and the pop-out archive.

  • Photo on the left with the receipt under it: To buy with prices, then In your Inventory with the amounts this recipe uses.
  • Every price is the sale price, else the flyer price, else the median (see Meal pricing.md). The receipt totals To buy, From inventory and This meal; the Prices switch hides them all.
  • Servings change on the receipt and rescale every amount and price. Anything you'd run short of moves to To buy.
  • Header: title, one fact line (time, serves, estimate, keeps), Prices bottom right, then a row of pills: Start cooking, Plan, Similar recipes, Open full recipe.
  • Steps use the Cook mode rail. Click a step to make it current; click again to mark it done. Timers run in place without opening Cook mode.
  • Notes at the bottom. Similar recipes turns the card into a list of recipes that share its ingredients (tap an ingredient to leave it out; Back returns to the recipe); the footer shows the top four.
