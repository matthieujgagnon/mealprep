What happens when you tap the box

Matt, Oct 13: "When you tap an item's box, it just disappears. You don't really see what you checked or where it went." Every board below is live. Tap the boxes, scroll the phone lists, and use Slow motion to watch each part.

  • A checked row never leaves the list. It stays where it is, tinted, until its store's Add to inventory takes it.
  • Each tap gives one confirmation on the row and one short trip to where the item is counted: the cart for ✓, the store footer for +.
  • Each sequence ends within 600 ms and never blocks the next tap. You can check the next item while the chip is still flying.
  • Every tap updates one shared toast with the item's name and Undo. It never stacks and stays 5 s after the last tap.
  • Phone: when the pink cart bar scrolls off the top, a small copy docks at the bottom, so the chip always has somewhere to land. Desktop: the cart card is sticky.
