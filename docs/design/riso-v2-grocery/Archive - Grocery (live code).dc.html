Grocery list

Built from every planned meal from today on, plus items added by hand. One grocery item (GroceryItem, "line" form) in By store, By aisle and By recipe; checkbox, name and meta line (brand, recipes, recipe quantity on a phone), then the fixed right columns: sale tag or "+ Inventory", recipe quantity, a round number, ×. The pink cart card and the green On sale card sit in the side column; on a phone a pink "I'm at the store" button opens Store mode.

VS HANDOFF (docs/design/grocery-item)
  • The quantity is a 42px round number (DM Mono 700 16px), not a 78 × 26 pill.
  • Store group header has no track-coloured band; it is the card's own surface with a 2px rule under it, and a small 26px round × for your own stores.
  • The right cluster's columns are 200 (sale tag / + Inventory), 112 (recipe quantity), 54 (number) and the 44px ×.
  • Phone held upright hides the sale tag; sideways it shows next to the number.
  • The cart card has a 8px hard shadow (handoff: 6px).
