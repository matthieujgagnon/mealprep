# Riso v2 (4 of 5): Shared finder and the Planner

Screenshots of the Planner with local test data (the recipes, Inventory shelves and planned week were put
in a throwaway database for the pictures; none of it is in the app, and the recipe photos are plain
colour tiles).

Desktop (1280 px), English:
- `planner-desktop-en.png`: the board (7 days by 3 meals, today's header pink, Saturday and Sunday in a pink dotted block) with the finder under it, closed to its search bar and filters. The "Weekend: Sat Sun" pill is above the board.
- `planner-desktop-en-weekend-setting.png`: the weekend pill open, the seven days to switch on or off.
- `planner-desktop-en-slot-card.png`: an empty slot clicked: its card with Recipe, Note and Nothing planned.
- `planner-desktop-en-finder.png`: the finder open with all the recipes.
- `planner-desktop-en-cook-with.png`: Cook with open: Expiring soon with days left, then the user's own shelves (Fridge, Freezer, Pantry and a custom Spice rack) as drawers, "1/6" on a drawer with a pick, "1 chosen" next to the ×, and the results using that pick.
- `planner-desktop-en-popout.png`: a recipe's pop-out: time and servings, meal, planned day, what you have, what to buy, steps, and the three buttons.
- `planner-desktop-en-main-meal.png`: Main meal: the yellow banner with its ingredients grouped, and the recipes that share them.
