# Recipes: the Cookbook as a bento grid, Imported as its own section

Screenshots of the Recipes page against `docs/design/riso-v2-recipes-bento/`.

- `desktop-en-*`: 1280 px wide, English. 1 is the top of the page (the Cookbook section opens with the big tile); 2 is the whole page: the Cookbook as a bento grid (the design's 8-tile pattern, then a last group of 3 closed with a 2x2 and two 2x1 so there is no gap) and, under it, Imported as the plain same-size photo cards; 3 is after pressing the Imported button, which scrolls to its section; 4 is a search with three results: they flow through the pattern again and the first one is the big tile.
- `phone-fr-*`: 390 px wide, French. 1 is the top; 2 the Cookbook two across (3:4, smaller title); 3 is after pressing « Importées »: the section's heading stops under the pinned header.

The recipe photos are plain gradients standing in for real ones (a recipe with no photo gets the plain paper-grey tile; the app never makes one up); the data is a test account's, made through the API. Re-take them with a local server and any test account that has about 11 Cookbook recipes and a few imported ones.
