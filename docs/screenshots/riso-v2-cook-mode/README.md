# Cook mode v2, the step view (design 2c)

Screenshots of the rebuilt step view against `docs/design/riso-v2-cook-mode/`.

- `desktop-en-*`: 1280 × 800, English. 1 and 2 are the design's state 5.1 (the step's timer waiting, nothing checked) in light and dark; 3 and 4 are 5.2 (timer running, two ingredients checked); 5 is the last step (Finish, no Up next, and the yellow chip for the timer still running on step 2); 6 is the finished view, which is still the earlier one and stays light after a dark step view.
- `phone-fr-*`: 390 × 844, French, the same states (the design's 5.3 is 4).
- `tablet-900-en-light.png`: between a phone and a wide screen the photo and timer drop under the step.

The recipe photo is a plain gradient standing in for a real one. The fonts are the browser's fallback sans because the build machine can't load Google Fonts; the app loads Bricolage Grotesque and DM Mono.
