# The recipe card (Riso v2)

Screenshots of the rebuilt recipe card against `docs/design/riso-v2-recipe-card/`.

- `desktop-en-*`: 1280 px wide, English. 1 is the whole card (the photo over the receipt, the header, the steps on the rail with their timer pills, Notes, and "Uses the same ingredients"); 2 has two timers running at once (step 1 done, step 2 current, with what it uses); 3 has step 1's timer at Time's up while step 2's keeps running; 4 is the card at 6 servings: every amount rescaled and the chicken you'd run short of moved to To buy as "150 G MORE"; 5 is the Similar view with Lemon left out; 6 is ← Back, with the servings, the current step and the timers as they were; 7 is the ⋯ menu.
- `phone-fr-*`: 390 px wide, French. 1 is the top with the bar fixed at the bottom; 2 the receipt; 3 a running timer under its step; 4 Notes and the footer cards that scroll sideways; 5 the Similar view (an ingredient left out); 6 ← Retour, the timer still running; 7 the ⋯ menu.

No price shows anywhere: prices wait for the Meal pricing ticket. The recipe photos are plain gradients standing in for real ones; the data is a test account's, made through the API.
